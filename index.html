Brief biography

I’m Adedoyin Adebola Samuel, a cybersecurity and ICT professional based in Abuja, Nigeria, and I’m studying for a B.Sc. in Software Engineering at Miva Open University. I already hold an M.Sc. in Cybersecurity and Forensics from Bingham University (2025) and a B.Sc. in Computer Science.

I founded ADYN Technologies, which I want to grow into a research-driven cybersecurity and digital forensics consultancy for organisations across Africa and beyond.

My research includes a quantitative cybersecurity risk assessment of MTN Nigeria, and I build practical tools too, such as an AI system that detects phishing and malicious email. I work by two rules: evidence comes before conclusions, and documentation is part of the deliverable. Away from the screen, I’m a founding member and media lead of Reach Out Dyslexia, which promotes inclusive education in Nigerian schools.
