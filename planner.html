    How the planner works

    1. Add a task

      Enter a title, course, type, priority and due date. Overdue tasks are flagged automatically.

    2. Track progress

      Move each task through To do, In progress, Review and Done. The checkbox and the status menu stay in sync.

    3. Get reminded

      Press Email for a reminder in your inbox, or Calendar for an alert at 9:00 the day before the due date.

    4. Review your week

      Use Email me my open tasks for a single summary of everything still to do, and Clear completed to tidy up.
